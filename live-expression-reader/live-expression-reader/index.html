How Gemma Adaptive Reader Works

Reads your facial comprehension signals in real time to adapt and re-create documents.

1. Insert Files & Read PDFs

Click Insert / Upload PDF to load any research paper, book chapter, or textbook. You can also pick from the preloaded sample documents.

2. Facial Comprehension Tracking

The top-right camera tracks your facial muscles (FACS) and cognitive state: corrugator furrowing (AU4) and eyelid tightening (AU7) indicate confusion; sustained focus indicates high comprehension.

3. Adaptive Re-Creation with Gemma

Whenever you struggle with a section, click ⚡ Re-Create with Gemma (or wait for the auto-confusion alert). Gemma will rewrite the document with intuitive real-world analogies, step-by-step breakdowns, and simplified terminology.

4. Chat with Gemma

Switch to the Gemma AI Discussion tab to ask any question about the document. Gemma is aware of your facial state and will guide you patiently.
